# Page Sections (v1)

One page, smooth scrolling, in this order:

1. **Header**
   Logo or shop name on the left, anchor links on the right (Collections, À propos, Contact). Slim and transparent.

2. **Hero**
   One striking visual, a short tagline, and one soft call to action ("Découvrir") that scrolls down. This section does most of the impressing.

3. **Notre histoire (À propos)**
   Two or three lines on who she is and why she works with flowers. Keeps the page personal.

4. **Nos créations**
   A small showcase of 4 to 6 bouquets or arrangements. Name and one-line description each. No prices, no buy button.

5. **Ce qui nous distingue**
   Three short points with minimal icons, for example fresh flowers, made by hand, delivered with care. To be adapted to what she actually offers.

6. **Phrase / citation**
   Full-width band with one elegant sentence. Gives the page room to breathe.

7. **Contact**
   Instagram, phone or WhatsApp, city or area. A "Nous écrire" link instead of a form.

8. **Footer**
   Copyright, social icons, optional line: "Bientôt : commandes en ligne".

## Notes
- Sections 5 and 6 are optional and can be merged or dropped to keep the page lean.
- Every section should work on a phone first.
